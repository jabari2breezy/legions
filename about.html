Who We Are

Legions is a youth volunteer group based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, run by secondary students, university students, and young professionals.

We are not an NGO, a corporation, or a government programme. We are a movement of young people who believe that immediate community needs deserve immediate action.

Why It Started

Instead of waiting on traditional NGOs or large aid organizations, local youth founded Legions to address immediate community needs directly.

We focus on practical, hands-on work: fixing run-down facilities, supporting vulnerable families, cleaning public spaces, and planting trees — without relying on corporate funding or heavy administration.

Our Values

Action first

Talk less, build more, show up early.

Radical transparency

Every donation is tracked and spent where it matters.

Youth leadership

Students and young professionals lead every project.
