Who We Are

Born from the streets we call home.

A youth volunteer group in Dar es Salaam, run by secondary students, university students, and young professionals who refuse to wait for permission to help their city.

How We Work

Direct labor. No ego. Peer power.

Direct Labor

Members show up in person and do the physical work, so donor money goes to materials, supplies, and food.

No Ego or Hierarchy

A flat collective where any young person can jump in, volunteer, and take ownership of projects.

Peer Fundraising

Micro-donations raised transparently via M-Pesa and Tigo Pesa, with zero administrative overhead.
