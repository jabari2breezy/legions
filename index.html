Who We Are

Born from the streets we call home.

Who We Are

Legions is a youth volunteer group based in Dar es Salaam, Tanzania, run by secondary students, university students, and young professionals.

Why It Started

Instead of waiting on traditional NGOs or large aid organizations, local youth founded Legions to address immediate community needs directly. They focus on practical, hands-on work: fixing run-down facilities, supporting vulnerable families, cleaning public spaces, and planting trees — without relying on corporate funding or heavy administration.

How They Work

A flat collective. Real boots on the ground.

Three principles keep Legions lean, transparent, and accountable — every shilling goes where it matters.

Direct Labor

Members show up in person to do the physical work, ensuring donor money goes entirely toward raw materials, supplies, and food.

No Ego or Hierarchy

A flat collective where any young person can jump in, volunteer, and take ownership of projects.

Peer Fundraising

Micro-donations are raised transparently across social media using local mobile money services (M-Pesa and Tigo Pesa) with zero administrative overhead.

Key Projects & Impact

Small team. Measurable change.

Project 01

AMSEN Special Needs Visits

Al Muntazir Special Needs Center

What They Did

Organized interactive art, music, and play sessions for students at the Al Muntazir Special Needs (AMSEN) center.

Impact

40+ students engaged 100% supplies donated 25+ volunteers trained

Purchased sensory art and learning supplies went entirely to the center's therapy department.

Project 02

Coastal Beach Cleanups

Coco Beach & Msasani

What They Did

Cleared heavily polluted shorelines around Coco Beach and Msasani, sorting waste on-site for proper recycling.

Impact

150+ volunteers mobilized 1.5+ tons removed

3,300+ lbs of plastic, glass, and trash cleared from the coastline.

Project 03

The Ramadhan Project

Citywide food drive

What They Did

Raised funds online to buy food in bulk directly from wholesale suppliers, assembled food packs, and hand-delivered them across target neighborhoods.

Impact

1,200+ fasting individuals reached 100% to food procurement

Hampers delivered to low-income families and local orphanages.

Project 04

Tree Planting — Project MYK

Public schools

What They Did

Planted native shade and fruit trees at unshaded public schools and established student-led watering teams to keep them alive.

Impact

500+ trees planted 90%+ survival rate

Hundreds of students benefiting through student guardianship.

Project 05

Ujasiri House Renovation

Tumaini La Maisha

What They Did

Plastered, repainted, and painted custom murals at Ujasiri House, a free hostel for pediatric cancer patients and their families at Muhimbili National Hospital.

Impact

30+ families served daily 100% volunteer labor

All funds went directly to paint and repair supplies — transforming the hostel environment.
